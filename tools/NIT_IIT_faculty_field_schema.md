# NIT / IIT Faculty Recruitment — Exhaustive Field Schema

Compiled from the **NIT Delhi faculty recruitment form (Advt. 08/2024)**, which implements **Schedule 'E' of the First Statutes of the NITs (Gazette of India No. 651, 24 July 2017, as amended 2023)**. This is the common framework across all NITs; IITs use a closely similar detailed format. Use this as the master list for the admin panel so any generated résumé maps directly to these committees' expectations.

> **Note on weightings:** credit-point values and caps below are quoted from the NIT Delhi 2024 form. Exact points/caps vary slightly between institutes, cadres (Asst./Assoc./Professor), and Schedule-E revisions, and are calculated *since last promotion*. The admin panel should store these as **configurable** rather than hard-coded.

---

## SECTION 1 — Personal & Application Details (form header)
- Post applied for (Professor / Associate Professor / Assistant Professor Grade I / Grade II)
- Department / Discipline
- Area(s) of specialization + micro-specialization
- Application reference number
- Full name (as per certificates)
- Father's / Mother's name
- Date of birth
- Gender
- Category (UR / EWS / SC / ST / OBC-NCL) + certificate details
- PwD status (yes/no, type, %)
- Nationality / OCI status
- Marital status
- Religion (where asked)
- Aadhaar / PAN / Passport no.
- Correspondence address, permanent address
- Email, mobile, alternate phone
- Passport-size photograph
- Scanned signature
- Whether currently/previously employed in Govt/PSU/Institute (for NOC / Proper Channel)
- Are you an internal/serving candidate? (yes/no)

## SECTION 2 — Academic Qualifications (Annexure A)
For **each** of: Post-Doc (if any), Ph.D., PG, UG, 10+2, 10th —
- Degree / examination
- Specialization / branch
- University / Institution
- Year of passing (and notification date for Ph.D.)
- CGPA / % (and class/division)
- Whether institution is UGC/AICTE/AIU recognized (and QS/THE rank if foreign)
- Ph.D. thesis title; supervisor(s)
- Certificate attached (Y/N) + page ref

## SECTION 3 — Teaching Experience (Annexure B)
Per position (regular basis only counts):
- Designation
- Organization / Institute
- Pay level / scale (AGP)
- From – To (and total duration)
- Nature (regular / contract / visiting — committees weigh regular)
- Supporting doc (appointment letter, experience cert, salary slip) + page ref

## SECTION 4 — Industry / Research Experience (Annexure C)
Per position:
- Designation / role (incl. Post-Doctoral Fellow)
- Organization
- From – To / duration
- Nature of work
- Supporting doc + page ref

## SECTION 5 — Credit-Point Categories (Annexures D1–D22)
*The exhaustive "everything counts" section. Each is a repeatable list.*

### D1(A) — Externally Sponsored R&D Projects (as PI / Co-PI)
*8 pts/project; PI gets 5, rest split among others.*
Fields per project: Title · Completed/Ongoing · Duration (From–To) · Funding agency · Amount (₹ Lakhs) · Role (PI/Co-PI) · No. of Co-PIs · Sanction-letter proof

### D1(B) — Patents Granted
*8 pts/patent.*
Fields: Patent name · Registration no. · Date of award/application · Awarding country · Status (filed/published/granted) · Co-awardee(s) · Are you main awardee (Y/N) · Grant-letter proof

### D2 — Consultancy Projects (as PI / Co-PI)
*2 pts per ₹5 Lakhs, max 10 pts.*
Fields: Sponsoring agency · Project title · Duration (From–To) · Consultancy amount · Role · Sanction-letter proof

### D3 — Ph.D. Theses Supervised (awarded / submitted)
*8 pts/student; 1st supervisor 5, rest split.*
Fields: Research scholar name · Thesis title · Status (Submitted/Awarded) · Date of submission/award · Role (Sole/1st/Co-supervisor) · No. of co-supervisors · Proof

### D4 — Journal Papers (SCI / Scopus indexed; paid journals not allowed)
*4 pts/paper; first author/main supervisor 2, rest split.*
Fields: Paper title · Journal name · Volume, pages, year · Indexed in (SCI/Scopus) · DOI · Role (First author/Main supervisor/Other) · No. of other authors · First-page proof

### D5 — Conference Papers (SCI / Scopus / WoS / renowned conference)
*1 pt/paper, max 10; first author 0.6, rest split.*
Fields: Paper title · Conference name · Volume, pages, year · Indexed in · Role · No. of other authors · Proof

### D6 — Senior Administrative Roles
*(HoD, Dean, Chief Warden, Prof In-charge T&P, Advisor Estate, CVO, PI Exam, TEQIP Coordinator) — 2 pts/semester, max 16.*
Fields: Position held · No. of semesters · Duration (From–To) · Proof/certificate

### D7 — Institutional Roles
*(Warden, Asst. Warden, Associate Dean, Chairman/Convener Institute Academic Committee, Faculty In-charge of Computer Centre/IT Services/Library/Admission/Student Activities) — 1 pt/semester, max 8.*
Fields: Position · No. of semesters · Duration · Proof

### D8 — Committee Roles
*(Chairman/Convener of standing & special committees; Faculty in-charge of units, 1-yr each) — 0.5 pt/semester, max 3.*
Fields: Position · No. of years · Duration · Proof

### D9 — Departmental Activities
*(Lab in-charge / department-level committee, min 1 year) — 0.5 pt/semester, max 3.*
Fields: Position · No. of years · Duration · Proof

### D10 — Workshops / FDPs / Short-Term Courses (min 5 days) as Coordinator/Convener
*2 pts/course, max 8.*
Fields: Event name/title · Duration (From–To) · No. of days · Proof

### D11 — National Programs (e.g. GIAN) as Course Coordinator
*2-week program: 2 pts (max 4); 1-week: 1 pt (max 2).*
Fields: Course title · Duration · No. of days · Proof

### D12 — National / International Conferences Organized (as Chairman/Secretary)
*3 pts/program, max 6.*
Fields: Conference title · National/International · Duration · Post held · Proof

### D13 — Length of Service Beyond Minimum Required (for cadre)
*2 pts/semester, max 10.*
Fields: Designation · Organization · Duration · No. of semesters · Proof

### D14 — Establishment of New Lab(s)
*Max 4 pts.*
Fields: Lab name(s) · Organization · Department · Year of establishment · Proof

### D15 — Theory Teaching Over & Above 6 Credit Hours
*1 pt/credit-hr, max 6.*
Fields: Academic year · Semester (Odd/Even) · Teaching load hrs/week (theory) · Extra load · Proof

### D16 — Post-Graduate Dissertations Guided
*0.5 pt/project, max 10.*
Fields: Dissertation/project title · Year · Student name · Proof

### D17 — Under-Graduate Dissertations / Projects Guided
*0.25 pt/project, max 4.*
Fields: Project title · Year · Student name · Proof

### D18 — Books: Text/Reference from Reputed **International** Publishers
Fields: Book title · Author(s) · Publisher · ISBN/ISSN · Year · Proof

### D19 — Books from Reputed **National** Publishers / Book **Chapters** in International-Publisher Books
Fields: Title · Chapter (if any) · Author(s) · Publisher · ISBN/ISSN · National/International · Year (From–To) · Proof

### D20 — Significant Outreach Activities Outside Institute
*(Common cap ~4 pts.)*
Fields: Nature of activity · Activity title · Period (From–To) · Department/Institute · Sponsoring agency (if any) · Proof

### D21 — Professional Fellowships
*(Fellow IEEE, FNA, FNAE, FNASc, etc.)*
Fields: Fellowship/body · Year · Membership/ID no. · Proof

### D22 — Placement Percentage (only for Placement Cell officers / Faculty In-charge)
*≥85%: max 20 pts; 75–84%: max 10 pts.*
Fields: Placement % · In the capacity of · Institute · Year · Proof

## SECTION 6 — Declaration & Checklist (Annexure E)
- Total number of enclosures + total pages
- Self-attestation declaration
- Signature, date, place
- Annexure checklist (A, B, C, D1–D22, E all attached)

## SECTION 7 — Additional CV Fields Commonly Required (good to capture)
*(Appear in IIT forms / NIT Patna-style sheets / general academic CVs and strengthen the résumé.)*
- **Professional body memberships** (body, membership grade, ID, since) — distinct from D21 fellowships
- **Citation metrics**: h-index, i10-index, total citations (Google Scholar / Scopus / WoS), with profile links
- **Awards & honours** (best paper/project, gold medals, national ranks like GATE)
- **Invited talks / keynotes / guest lectures / resource-person sessions** (event, host, date)
- **Editorial / reviewer roles** (journal/conference, role, period)
- **Collaborations / MoUs / international visits**
- **Funded student/startup mentorship, technology transfer / commercialization**
- **Courses developed / MOOCs / NPTEL** (title, platform, year)
- **Curriculum / syllabus development contributions**
- **Research statement** (vision + future plan) and **Teaching statement / teaching plan** (courses you can teach) — Warangal and others require these as separate uploads
- **List of references / referees** (name, designation, affiliation, contact)

---

### How this maps to the admin panel
- Each D-category becomes a **repeatable section** (add/remove rows) with its own fields + an optional **credit-points** auto-calc using configurable per-item weight and cap.
- The panel can output **two résumé "views" from one dataset**: (a) the clean recruiter/web résumé we already have, and (b) a **Schedule-E credit-point summary** (Annexure-D-style table with running totals) that matches what NIT/IIT committees score.
- Weightings/caps stored in a small `creditRules` config so they can be tuned per institute without code changes.
